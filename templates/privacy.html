01

Introduction

Welcome to Study_Mate. Study_Mate is an AI-powered study platform designed to help students understand concepts, practice their knowledge, and organize their study time.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use Study_Mate.

By creating an account or using Study_Mate, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

When you create an account, Study_Mate may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

Account information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password or password credentials
  • Class or grade level
  • Targeted or preferred subject
  • Previous academic score or percentage

Learning information

Depending on the features you use, Study_Mate may process information related to your learning activity, such as:

  • Topics you ask about
  • Questions submitted to the AI
  • Quiz attempts and results
  • Study schedules
  • Tasks and learning goals
  • General progress information

Technical information

The platform or its hosting infrastructure may automatically receive limited technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address, and basic access logs.

03

How We Use Information

Study_Mate uses collected information primarily to provide and improve the study experience.

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in.
  • Personalize explanations and learning content.
  • Generate quizzes based on learning topics.
  • Help organize study schedules and tasks.
  • Track learning progress and performance.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the service.
  • Detect abuse, security problems, or unauthorized access.

Study_Mate does not need your personal information for purposes unrelated to providing, securing, or improving the service unless you are informed otherwise.

04

AI Features

Study_Mate uses artificial intelligence to provide features such as explanations, quizzes, and personalized study assistance.

Information you submit to an AI feature may be processed by the AI service used by Study_Mate in order to generate a response.

Important

AI-generated responses can contain mistakes. Study_Mate should be used as a learning aid and not as a replacement for teachers, parents, guardians, textbooks, or other trusted educational resources.

Study_Mate should not be used to submit highly sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, or information about another person without appropriate permission.

05

Information Sharing

Study_Mate does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the platform. For example, third-party infrastructure or AI providers may process information required to provide specific features.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal requests.
  • Protect the security of Study_Mate or its users.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or unauthorized use.
  • Protect the rights and safety of users or others.
06

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, secure connections, and appropriate database security practices.

No system is completely secure.

Although reasonable security measures are used, no website, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized, subject to technical, legal, or operational requirements.

08

Cookies

Study_Mate may use cookies or similar technologies required for essential functions such as maintaining login sessions and remembering necessary preferences.

If additional analytics or optional cookies are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to explain their purpose.

09

Children's Privacy

Study_Mate is designed for students and may be used by children and teenagers.

If a user is under the age required to provide valid consent under the laws applicable to them, they should use Study_Mate with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian where required.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

10

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include the ability to:

  • Request access to information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account information, subject to applicable requirements.
  • Ask questions about how your information is processed.

To make a privacy request, contact Study_Mate using the contact information provided below.

11

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Study_Mate develops new features, changes its technology, or as applicable privacy requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
